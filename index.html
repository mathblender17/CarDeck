CarDeck is a free car stereo for your browser. Load your own music or a YouTube link, and drive.

Owner's manual, short version.

One unit, six discs and a display that actually listens to the song. There is nothing to sign up for.

Load a disc

Press OPEN / CLOSE and pick a folder of music, or paste a YouTube link. Six slots, like a real disc changer.

Watch it listen

The spectrum reads the real audio from your files. YouTube bars are simulated, and the display says so.

Your files stay yours

Everything plays on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.

Best on a desktop

Made for Chrome or Edge on a big screen. It fits a phone, but the keys get small.
